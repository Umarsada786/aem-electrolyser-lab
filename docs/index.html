03 / Inside the model

Electrochemistry

V = Erev + activation + ohmic + temperature correction + optional transport loss. Nernst pressure dependence and an effective inverse-hyperbolic-sine activation term form a reduced-order polarization model.

Mass & energy

Faraday’s law predicts H₂, O₂ and water consumption. Electrical power, LHV efficiency, specific electricity use and an HHV-based heat balance follow from the same operating point.

What is not validated

Thermal dynamics, degradation, gas crossover, membrane hydration, catalyst-specific kinetics, pressure dynamics and balance-of-plant performance. The Python thermal utility is illustrative only.

Hydrogen flow is almost proportional to current in the source data; its high agreement is not independent proof of Faradaic efficiency. The workbook does not establish pressure reference, oxygen pressure, gas normalization conditions, cell count or active area. Explicit assumptions are used.

Method background: Chang, Gu & Chan (2024), Energies 17, 5682. This implementation is a simplified effective model, not a reproduction of that paper.
